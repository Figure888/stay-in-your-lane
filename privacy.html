Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Stay in Your Lane ("the app") does with your information. The app is published by [YOUR LLC NAME] ("we", "us"). If you have questions, email [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

Short version: we don't run accounts, we don't collect personal information, and we don't sell or share anything about you. Your game data stays on your device. Purchases are handled by Google Play, not by us.

Information we collect

None that identifies you. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or device identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally, in your browser or app storage, so the game works when you reopen it:

  • Your play-money chip balance
  • Game settings and progress
  • A record that you confirmed you are 18 or older

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. We cannot recover it for you.

Purchases

The app sells packs of play-money chips through Google Play Billing. When you buy a pack, Google processes the transaction. We never see or store your payment card, billing address, or Google account details. Google provides us with a purchase confirmation so the app can add the chips to your balance.

Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Play-money chips have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, redeemed, transferred to another player, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes of any kind. All purchases are final except where Google Play's refund policy applies.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not build profiles, and there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

The app simulates casino-style card play and is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. If you believe a child has used the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or share information about you, because we do not hold any. The only third party involved is Google, which processes purchases and app distribution as described above.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You control the app's local data directly by clearing its storage on your device. For purchase records, contact Google Play support.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS. Local game data is stored using standard device storage and is protected by your device's own security. No data is transmitted to servers we operate.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if we add accounts, online multiplayer, or analytics — we will update this page and change the date at the top before the change ships.
